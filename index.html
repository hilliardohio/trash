Methodology: Household counts are known for only two billing periods — Nov-2025 (10,739 = 8,062 RESI + 2,677 SENIOR curb-service units, per LWS invoice #1071703) and May-2026 (10,796 = 8,143 RESI + 2,653 SENIOR, per LWS invoice #1147620). All other months are linearly interpolated/extrapolated at the implied rate of +9.5 households/month, so per-household figures for those months are estimates, not billed counts. Pounds = tons × 2,000, divided by estimated households for that month. Brush & leaf pick-up tonnage is estimated from chipper-truck loads (small chipper 6 CY/load, big chipper 16 CY/load) at an assumed 600 lbs per cubic yard for green chips fresh from the chipper, covering the Apr 13 – Jul 27, 2026 season to date; the Monthly Detail table lists the April–June figures that align with the solid-waste months (July's 10.80 tons appears in the chart above).
